About

Am I the right person for your project?
Let's find out.

I don't have a standard background, and honestly? That's my biggest asset. My design process is shaped by a life of constant reinvention. I learned resilience in Cuba, business strategy in Chile where I co-founded Somos Arte and built a community of 10,000+ people from scratch, and adaptability in France, where I moved for love and mastered a third language.

Why does this matter to you?
Because I don't look at a project and just see colors and typography. Thanks to my entrepreneurial roots, I see business goals, constraints, and user needs. I understand that a design isn't finished when it looks good in Figma it's finished when it works in the browser and converts for your business.

I bridge the gap between creative vision and technical reality. Whether it's coding a prototype, modeling in Blender, or defining a brand identity, I give purpose to every pixel.
